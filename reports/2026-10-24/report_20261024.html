We present VERA, an open-source self-hosted dashboard deployed to facilitate responsible AI processes within a large European bank. A reviewer selects a model and runs public benchmarks against it. We run four served open-weight models through the requirement pipeline, and the dashboard displays a score for each measurable requirement. A within-subject study then compares how participants read these benchmark results: from the raw output files in one condition, and through the VERA dashboard in the other. 4. Automated Enrichment Analysis with GraphRAG: Efficient Multi-Database ORA Extraction and Biological Interpretation

Over-representation analysis (ORA) is widely used to interpret gene-expression studies, but its outputs are often fragmented across gene-set databases and presented as flat term lists that obscure gene-level links among related enrichment signals. We propose Graph-Based Over-Representation Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GBOR-RAG), an integrated and traceable framework for ORA interpretation. GBOR-RAG represents heterogeneous gene-set databases as a unified term--gene knowledge graph, where shared genes connect related terms across sources. After graph-based ORA scoring, the framework retrieves a compact connected evidence subgraph that preserves both cross-database relationships and gene-level support. The subgraph conditions an LLM to generate biological interpretations, and we introduce a claim-level faithfulness metric to assess whether generated claims are supported by explicit evidence. Experiments show that graph-based ORA preserves conventional ORA results with high consistency (mean Pearson $r=0.997$) while reducing query time by a median of $37\times$ (up to $48\times$). The retrieved subgraphs average 36.8 nodes and achieve the highest overall integration score (4.12), and GBOR-RAG attains the highest claim-level faithfulness (0.90) among the compared systems. Adding a PubMed literature layer further improves the overall interpretation quality from 4.17 to 4.75.

5. GigaBench: Benchmarking Learnable Representations for Gigapixel Image Compression and Super-Resolution

Learnable Representations (LRs) can fit parametric families of functions to diverse kinds of signals. These emergent neural-native data formats are gathering interest in the scientific community due to their compactness, resolution-agnosticism and amenability to direct neural processing. On the other hand, gigapixel images are becoming increasingly widespread across a variety of scientific, cultural, and industrial domains. In this paper, we present GigaBench, the first benchmark and experimental evaluation focused on deploying LRs with gigapixel imagery. GigaBench includes images collected, acquired, and synthetically rendered across diverse domains, and addresses image compression and super-resolution. We adapt a representative set of LRs, spanning Implicit, Hybrid, and Explicit methods, so as to operate reliably at the gigapixel scale on consumer hardware. Through systematic evaluation across reconstruction quality, model size, compression and inference times, we identify consistent trends across methods and suggest actionable guidance for future investigation.

4. headroom 压缩工具输出与日志，JSON 场景省 60–95% token

开源项目 headroom 的定位是在内容进入 LLM 之前做压缩，覆盖工具输出、日志、文件以及 RAG 检索分块。按项目给出的数据，编码 agent 场景可减少约 20% 的 token，JSON 类内容的 token 可减少 60–95%，同时答案质量保持不变。它以三种形态提供：代码库（library）、代理（proxy）和 MCP server，从而在不同接入方式下都能插入模型调用前的链路。社区侧数据为 77,672 stars、周增 2,206、6,031 forks、607 个 open issues。上下文长度与 token 成本是 agent 产品化的硬约束，而在调用模型前压缩这一层无需更换模型或重写 prompt，就能直接压低成本，真实 agent 链路中最大的上下文膨胀往往来自工具返回的大段 JSON 与日志。
