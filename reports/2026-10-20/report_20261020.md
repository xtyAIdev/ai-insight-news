### 2. Preference Is Not Position: Evaluating Reranking on NevIR in Fixed Hybrid Candidate Pools

On 2,736 Test query directions, a pairwise-trained ranker improves strict per-query preference over fixed fusion (56.62% versus 52.30%) while reducing preferred-passage mean reciprocal rank (MRR) from 0.5764 to 0.3055.
