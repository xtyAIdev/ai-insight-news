1. BitMTP: When Multi-Token Prediction Meets Low-Bit Large Language Models

For efficient deployment of large language models (LLMs), methods of ultra-low-bit backbones reduce weight storage, memory traffic, and linear-layer execution cost, while multi-token prediction (MTP) methods reduce sequential decoding steps by predicting multiple future tokens in parallel. Although complementary, the direct combination of these methods cannot accelerate decoding at comparable quality: masked drafting lacks intermediate token input supplied in next-token training, while wider computation and causal key-value (KV) updates can offset the savings from fewer steps. To address these challenges, we propose BitMTP, an algorithm-system co-design framework for efficient MTP on ultra-low-bit LLM backbones. A joint next-token prediction and MTP objective equips a single model with both autoregressive (AR) generation and block-causal drafting. Confidence-only decoding, speculative-only decoding, and hybrid decoding provide different quality-throughput operating points without using separate draft and verifier models. Accepted-prefix alignment keeps the committed prefix, materialized causal KV boundary, and CUDA Graph replay state consistent for continued generation. BitMTP uses this contract to select width-specialized low-bit execution and reuse captured graphs over shared KV storage. 2. Efficient Edge-aware Attention Network for Graph Generation

Graph generative models commonly use graph-specific Transformer architectures to jointly update node and edge features. However, these architectures do not directly benefit from recent hardware-aware attention implementations, limiting their scalability. The adaptation is, however, non-trivial: edge and graph-level features must be injected into pairwise attention scores, graph masks must be respected, and dynamic edge updates remain a major source of quadratic memory usage. We propose an edge-aware attention layer for graph generation that incorporates structural information through head-wise score modulation, while updating edge representations with a lightweight residual mechanism that retains attention-score dependent edge updates. This layer can be effectively implemented using PyTorch's FlexAttention. We achieve similar generation quality to our baseline while reducing peak GPU memory by around 65% and substantially improving training and sampling throughput. These results suggest that the proposed architecture is a practical step toward scalable graph foundation models.

3. NarraSpace: Multimodal Scene-Grounded Evidence for Picture-Description Dementia Classification

Picture-description dementia classification uses a shared visual scene, but a transcript-level prediction alone does not identify the scene evidence underlying its inputs. NarraSpace links words and speech times to named picture elements and retrieves utterances for predefined scene events. Narrative-spatial descriptors summarize coverage and temporal organization. A frozen language model contrasts each event's text under examples from Alzheimer's disease and healthy-control training participants, alongside transcript surprisal and coverage measurements. Retained source records link event scores to their source utterances, speech intervals, and lexical matches. On the official 48-participant test set of the Alzheimer's Dementia Recognition through Spontaneous Speech benchmark, a fixed support vector classifier achieves $80.88\%\pm2.56\%$ mean accuracy over fifty seeds; averaged probabilities yield $81.25\%$ accuracy and $91.32\%$ area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. NarraSpace makes the construction of scene-linked classification inputs explicit and inspectable by connecting event-indexed language-model measurements to their source utterances, speech timing, and extraction records. Such source-linked measurements could support clinician review of picture descriptions by presenting quantitative summaries alongside the original speech evidence.

4. Benchmarking Arabic Dialects for Math Problems

Mathematical reasoning remains underexplored for low-resource languages, including Dialectal Arabic. To bridge this gap, we introduce the DAR-MGSM (Dialectal Arabic Multilingual Grade School Math) Benchmark, the first manually curated benchmark for evaluating mathematical reasoning in Dialectal Arabic. DAR-MGSM consists of math word problems (MWPs) requiring multi-step reasoning and covers five major Arabic dialects alongside Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). The benchmark comprises two parallel versions: translated-only and translated &amp; culturally localized. The latter version adapts entities and references to regional varieties. We evaluate eleven Arabic-centric and multilingual large language models on DAR-MGSM. The results show that the performance generally declines on the localized version, highlighting the challenges introduced by culturally grounded adaptations. However, the magnitude of this effect varies across models, with Arabic-centric models exhibiting different levels of sensitivity to localization. A mechanistic interpretability analysis shows that translated and localized variants of the same MWPs often induce distinct internal reasoning trajectories, even when aggregate accuracy remains similar. Overall, our results show that mathematical reasoning performance depends on both linguistic variety and cultural context.

5. Capable Teacher, Myopic Scorer: Reward-Free Teacher Repair for Agentic On-Policy Distillation

Agentic on-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student agent on its own trajectories while a teacher scores every token. A natural explanation of where it goes wrong is compounding error: the student's mistakes carry its trajectories beyond the region where the teacher is reliable, so recent methods keep training inside that region, through episode-length curricula, teacher-guided turns, prefix replay, or teacher intervention where the two disagree. We show this explanation is incomplete. From the states where the student departs most from the teacher, the teacher's own takeovers still finish the task, yet the student trained on its token scores keeps running out of turns. We attribute this to the myopia of next-token scoring. The teacher rates each token without seeing where the episode ends, so its scores let through the safe, delaying moves that outcome-based RL would punish with zero reward, a penalty agentic OPD lacks. We call this difference between what the teacher does and what it scores the rollout-scorer gap. We propose Rollout-to-Scorer Distillation (R2S), which repairs the scorer before distillation by mining high-disagreement states from the untrained student, running teacher takeovers from them, keeping the states where enough takeovers pass a reward-free check such as consensus or an LLM judge, and fine-tuning the teacher to give its takeover-informed scores on every assistant token from the divergence turn onward, without the takeover in context. 1. BitMTP：多token预测与超低比特LLM的算法系统协同设计

针对超低比特骨干虽能降低权重存储、内存流量与线性层开销，而多token预测（MTP）虽能并行预测多个未来token以减少串行解码步数、但两者直接拼接反而无法在同等质量下加速的问题，作者提出 BitMTP 这一面向超低比特 LLM 骨干的高效 MTP 算法-系统协同设计框架。其联合的 next-token prediction 与 MTP 目标让单一模型同时具备自回归生成与 block-causal drafting 能力，并提供 confidence-only、speculative-only 与 hybrid 三种解码方式，在不额外引入独立 draft 与 verifier 模型的前提下切换质量-吞吐工作点。accepted-prefix alignment 保证已提交前缀、已物化的 causal KV 边界与 CUDA Graph 重放状态在续写时保持一致，框架据此选择按宽度特化的低比特执行并在共享 KV 存储上复用已捕获的计算图。在 NVIDIA H20 上，采用 hybrid 解码的 BitMTP 于 BitNet b1.58-2B 上取得五任务平均 2.2 倍加速，在 1.7B-4B 的 Bonsai 模型上为 2.1-2.4 倍，且任务质量相当；原文另提及 RTX 3090 与 Jetson 平台上的结果，但材料在此处截断。论文数据同时显示该工作获 NeurIPS 2026 录用，作者数为 5；其意义在于把解码算法与低比特训练-推理的协同而非单点量化或单点投机解码，作为压缩显存带宽与生成延迟的共同抓手。

把量化与多token预测放在同一个目标函数和同一套 KV/CUDA Graph 契约下求解，绕开了“masked drafting 缺中间 token 输入、更宽计算与 causal KV 更新抵消步数收益”这对结构性矛盾，2 倍级加速是在质量相当条件下取得的，说明低比特的价值可以被解码侧真正兑现。若这一协同范式能延续到更长序列与更小显存场景，高效推理的竞争重心可能从“谁的量化位数更低”转向“谁的训练-推理-解码联合设计更紧”。

4. 阿语方言数学推理基准DAR-MGSM：本地化版本性能普遍下滑

5. Capable Teacher, Myopic Scorer: Reward-Free Teacher Repair for Agentic On-Policy Distillation

Agentic on-policy distillation（OPD）让学生在自身轨迹上训练、由教师逐 token 打分；主流解释把失效归因于误差累积，于是近期方法用 episode 级课程、教师引导轮次、prefix replay、分歧处教师介入，把训练限制在教师可靠区域内，而这篇两位作者的论文认为该解释并不完整。从学生偏离教师最远的状态出发，教师自己接管仍能完成任务，但用教师 token 分数训练的学生依旧会耗尽轮次。作者把原因归于 next-token 打分的近视：教师看不到 episode 在哪里结束，因而放行那些 outcome-based RL 会判零奖励的安全拖延动作，而 agentic OPD 缺少这一惩罚；这种教师做得到、其评分却放过的落差被命名为 rollout-scorer gap。为此提出的 Rollout-to-Scorer Distillation（R2S）先修评分器再蒸馏：从未训练的学生处挖掘高分歧状态，执行教师接管，保留足够多接管能通过无奖励检查（如共识或 LLM judge）的状态，再微调教师，使其在分歧轮起的每个 assistant token 上给出接管知情分数，而

论文指出 agentic on-policy distillation 的失败原因不只是学生轨迹跑出教师可靠区域造成的复合误差：即使轨迹仍在可靠区域内，教师逐 token 打分本身也是“短视”的（myopic scorer），会给出误导性监督。作者据此提出无需奖励模型的教师修复（reward-free teacher repair）方法来校正该打分。
