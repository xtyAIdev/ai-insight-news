1. GLOBE: Evolving Structured Agentic Memory for E-commerce Product Optimization in Generative Search

Optimizing product listings for generative search requires language agents not only to rewrite effectively but also to continually self-improve by retaining successful strategies across diverse categories. However, existing approaches often treat structured listings as free-form text, leaving effective strategies implicit in prompts, search trajectories, or rewritten outputs rather than accumulating them as a reusable agentic memory. To address this limitation, we introduce GLOBE, an agentic framework that drives continual self-improvement by storing optimization knowledge in an evolving handbook of explicit, field-scoped behaviors. In each episode, an Editor applies relevant behaviors and receives rank-based feedback, which a Reflector converts into behavior updates. Crucially for dependable execution in real-world settings, a Moderator removes redundant proposals, a Curator commits accepted changes, and consistently harmful behaviors are deactivated. This cycle transforms episodic feedback into a persistent, field-scoped optimization memory. Experiments show that structured behaviors outperform monolithic prompt optimization, while feedback-driven evolution and moderation further improve effectiveness and reduce downside risk. The learned behaviors also remain effective on unseen product categories, suggesting that agentic memory evolution supports interpretable and transferable product optimization.

2. Towards High Semantic Fidelity: Hyperdimensional Symbolic Messages in Multi-Agent Communication

Semantic fidelity is essential for multi-agent communication under non-ideal channels, comprising transmission fidelity and conversion fidelity. Yet existing numerical and neural message formats cannot simultaneously achieve both. To address this, we propose a novel message format-\textbf{H}yperdimensional \textbf{S}ymbolic \textbf{M}essages (HSM) and its corresponding communication framework \textbf{Herm}. For transmission fidelity, Herm suppresses semantic entanglement via an entity-attribute-value structured encoding pipeline. For conversion fidelity, Herm first leverages a receiver-centric ego-binding mechanism to align semantics. Then, a similarity-aware dual-branch fusion module is proposed to prevent critical semantic dilution. To realize explicit message-semantic conversion, we devise an iterative decoding scheme via algebraic inverse operations. Experiments exhibit that Herm outperforms baselines across various noisy scenarios while maintaining superior structural readability.

3. Behavioral Couplings: From Formation to Behavioral Emergence in Language Models

Fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) can induce behaviors beyond those explicitly targeted. For example, training a model to respond more warmly can make it more sycophantic, and training it to write insecure code can cause broad misalignment on prompts unrelated to coding. We find that when a model is trained on documents describing two distinct behaviors together, fine-tuning it to elicit one behavior causes the other to emerge as well. 4. AI-Driven Reproducibility and Analytical Robustness in Scientific Discovery

Reproducible and analytically robust research findings are essential to scientific progress, but evaluating these properties typically requires costly manual effort. We investigate whether LLM agents can help automate reproduction and robustness analysis, using a medical case study grounded in the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) database, a widely used U.S. cancer registry underlying tens of thousands of published studies. First, we introduce SEER-Repro, a benchmark of 20 published SEER-based papers together with the data and resources required to reproduce their findings. Second, we create an AI agent that successfully reproduces 77.4% of 2,190 atomic claims, while providing executable baselines for further analysis. Third, we develop an agentic analysis pipeline to test the robustness of the medical findings by identifying analytical choices underlying each finding, proposing defensible alternatives, and rerunning the analysis to determine whether those choices affect the conclusion. Across published papers in SEER-Repro, our pipeline identifies potentially consequential analytical choices in 9.5% of fully reproduced findings and flags them for expert review. These results show how LLM agents can support scientific review by reproducing findings, systematically testing their robustness, and directing expert attention toward potentially consequential analytical choices.

5. DiscoveryRSI: Recursively Learning to Discover Valuable Hypotheses

Scientific discovery requires developing worthwhile hypotheses into practical investigations, yet learning from this evolving research process remains challenging. We introduce an operational definition of valuable and testable research hypotheses: evidence-grounded claims about consequential uncertainties, coupled with feasible investigations, explicit decision criteria, and alternative explanations. 1. GLOBE 将电商商品优化策略沉淀为可进化的结构化记忆

EMNLP 2026 录用的论文 GLOBE（4 位作者）指出，面向生成式搜索的商品信息优化不仅要求语言智能体有效改写，还需跨品类保留成功策略以持续自我改进；而现有做法把结构化商品页当成自由文本，让有效策略散落在 prompt、搜索轨迹或改写结果里，无法积累为可复用的智能体记忆。GLOBE 的做法是把优化知识写入一份持续演化的手册，按字段范围显式记录行为：每轮由 Editor 调用相关行为并获得基于排名的反馈，Reflector 将反馈转成行为更新，Moderator 剔除冗余提议、Curator 提交被接受的修改，持续有害的行为则被停用，从而把单轮反馈转化为持久的分字段优化记忆。实验显示，结构化行为优于单体式 prompt 优化，反馈驱动的演化与审核机制进一步提升效果并降低下行风险；学到的行为在未见过的商品品类上同样有效，说明这种记忆演化支持可解释、可迁移的商品优化。也就是说，智能体记忆正从隐式 prompt 与轨迹中独立出来，成为一等结构化资产，这决定了商品能否被 AI 购物助手正确理解与推荐。

把策略从 prompt 中搬到可审计、可停用的行为手册，是 memory engineering 取代 prompt engineering 的一个具体样本。若这类显式记忆真能跨品类复用，电商优化服务商的护城河可能从“会调词”转向“攒下的行为库”，冷启动的重复试错成本也会被摊薄。

2. Towards High Semantic Fidelity: Hyperdimensional Symbolic Messages in Multi-Agent Communication

论文指出，非理想信道下的多智能体通信需要同时保证两类语义保真度——传输保真度与转换保真度，而现有数值型与神经型消息格式无法两者兼得。为此作者提出超维符号消息格式HSM及配套通信框架Herm。在传输侧，Herm通过实体-属性-值（entity-attribute-value）结构化编码流程抑制语义纠缠；在转换侧，它先用以接收端为中心的自绑定（ego-binding）机制对齐语义，再用相似性感知的双分支融合模块避免关键语义被稀释，并通过代数逆运算的迭代解码方案实现显式的消息—语义转换。实验显示，Herm在多种噪声场景下均优于基线，同时保持了更好的结构可读性；该论文已被NeurIPS 2026录用，作者共6人。把消息格式本身当作保真度的设计对象，意味着语义通信的改进空间可能部分从模型能力转向协议与编码结构，这对噪声与带宽受限下的多智能体通信及分布式AI系统具有直接参考价值。
