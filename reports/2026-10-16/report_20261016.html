1. Robust Multi-Agent Pursuit with Preemptive Communication via Reachability-Based Control Barrier Functions

Safe multi-agent coordination typically assumes what real-world, deployed, resource-constrained robots cannot provide: continuous, perfect state sharing. Robust control strategies must be complemented with robust communication strategies to deliver resilient deployments. In this work, we propose a unified control/communication strategy that provides safe control to guarantee inter-agent safety under intermittent communication and demonstrate its effectiveness in a pursuit-evasion scenario. Each agent computes the reachable set of neighboring agents based on the last state information received, then solves for a safe control input based on control barrier functions to ensure agents avoid collision. We prove that inter-agent collision avoidance is guaranteed provided communication staleness remains bounded. As the reachable set inflates between state transmissions, each agent reasons about when to preemptively transmit its state information to neighboring agents based on novel metrics characterizing uncertainty risk and kinematic risk. We implement and simulate a variety of pursuit-evasion scenarios and show that preemptive communication reduces messages by 77.5% compared to periodic and event-triggered baselines with zero collisions.

3. To Think or Not to Think: Pre-Decisional Reasoning Budgets for Referring Audio-Visual Segmentation

4. MetaShare: Failure-Aware Collective Self-Improvement for Open-Ended Agents

Humans often learn fastest not by repeating success, but by remembering which failures not to repeat. Open-ended self-improving agents can share experience across lineages, as in GEA, or edit their own improvement mechanisms, as in DGM-Hyperagents, yet failed improvement attempts are rarely retained as reusable cross-branch knowledge, and search can collapse onto redundant strategies. We introduce MetaShare, a failure-aware framework that records successful and unsuccessful agent modifications together with signed performance deltas. Before each generation, MetaShare retrieves diverse high-performing strategies alongside high-confidence failures from other lineages, letting agents reuse effective mechanisms while avoiding known dead ends. A task-blind auditor periodically injects absent or underrepresented mechanism categories, while embedding-based de-ranking limits redundancy during retrieval. Multiple meta-agents propose modifications in parallel at heterogeneous temperatures behind a deterministic selector that gates applicability, scope, compilability, and size. Experiments across paper review, coding, math grading, robotics reward design, and financial audit-risk assessment show that MetaShare's median matches or exceeds DGM-Hyperagents' final performance and reaches its own best-performing agent in 1.7--2.9$\times$ fewer evolution iterations.

👀 Watchlist

The rise of the agent memory and context-compression layer — claude-mem targets persistent context across sessions, while headroom compresses tool outputs, logs, files and RAG chunks before they hit the model. Both attack the same bottleneck: context windows and inference cost are what limit agent scale today. Whoever standardizes this layer first owns the entry point to the agent ecosystem.
Open-source computer-use and autonomous execution frameworks — cua and OpenHands both let agents operate real software and desktop environments directly — the leap from talking to actually doing work. This is where enterprise automation starts displacing meaningful amounts of human labor, and where permissioning, auditability and safety governance will surface first.
On-device open-weight models and AI hardware deployment — Meta's open-weight model runs locally on Macs, Alibaba's Qwen models were approved for China-market iPhones, and Qwen glasses plus Rivian in-car agents round out the picture: models are moving into devices along several routes at once. On-device compute, privacy compliance and hardware form factor will together decide the next consumer entry point.
Anthropic's trimmed IPO valuation expectations — A cut from roughly $2T to $1.5T is an early sign that pricing for top AI labs is normalizing. Watch whether this is an isolated adjustment or the start of a sector-wide re-rating, and how it propagates into private-market terms, compute commitments and talent compensation.
Turning reasoning-budget and model-efficiency research into products — Pre-decisional reasoning budgets for when to think, RecSoup's merging approach for multi-objective recommendation, and MetaShare's failure-aware self-improvement all aim at steadier output for less compute. Once engineered into products, this line of work directly lowers per-task agent cost — a precondition for commercialization at scale.

2. RecSoup 用剪枝与参数合并实现免重训多目标权衡

推荐系统常需同时平衡多个相互冲突的目标，可控多目标推荐（CMOR）要求在推理阶段动态调整目标权衡、无需重训即可适应用户或系统需求的变化。已有 CMOR 方法依赖输出层的重排序或参数生成模块，前者限制了可行权衡的范围，后者带来可观的训练与推理开销；模型合并虽能通过参数组合复用多个单目标模型，但直接用于推荐系统会因缺乏共享线性区间与跨目标干扰而失效。RecSoup 提出轻量两阶段方案：先用同时考虑单目标重要性与跨目标相关性的剪枝策略缓解目标间的参数干扰，再借助可学习的偏好到系数映射完成自适应参数合并，从而在不联合重训、不引入生成模块的前提下在推理时动态控制权衡。理论上该工作证明了 RecSoup 在效果与效率上优于参数生成范式，能够不受平滑性约束地走遍完整 Pareto 前沿，并在多个基准上开展了实验（论文已被 CIKM 2026 录用，作者 3 人）。若这一结论在实际业务中成立，则业务方改变目标偏好将无需重训模型或增加服务延迟
