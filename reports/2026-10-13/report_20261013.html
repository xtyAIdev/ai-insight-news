1. Goal-Oriented Bundle Management and Flow Control for Deep-Space Communications via Reinforcement Learning

Deep-space communications operate under intermittent connectivity, long propagation delays, and relay storage constraints. In such networks, the Bundle Protocol enables store-carry-forward communication, requiring relay nodes to manage limited storage and scarce contact opportunities. Relay-side scheduling and dropping decisions are inherently goal-oriented: transmission resources should be allocated to bundles most valuable to downstream mission objectives. Conventional schedulers, such as FIFO, do not explicitly optimize task utility and may provide unfair service across heterogeneous planetary sources. This paper presents a closed-loop modeling and learning framework for relay-side bundle and flow management in a contact-driven deep-space network, where lunar and Martian updates are forwarded through a shared-buffer relay to Earth. We quantify destination-side information freshness using excess age $Δ_i^e(t)=[Δ_i(t)-d_i(t)]^+$, where $Δ_i(t)$ denotes the Age of Information of source $i$ and $d_i(t)$ is a source-dependent delay baseline. Based on this metric, we define a goal-oriented loss that supports source-dependent nonlinear penalties, allowing prioritization according to mission relevance. We formulate relay control as a constrained semi-Markov decision process (CSMDP) and solve it using masked Lagrangian Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). 2. Can Language Models Play the Wikipedia Game?

Language models increasingly support or automate information retrieval from document collections too large to index. This task is difficult when it requires specialist linguistic or scientific knowledge. We used the Wikipedia Game to test language models on an analogous information retrieval task. In the game, players must reach a target article using only article links. We compared sentence embedding models and LLMs across five Wikipedia languages and five academic-literature datasets. We construct very large local graphs of Wikipedia pages and of paper citations using OpenAlex and provide code to build and navigate them. This allows the study of language models' navigation capabilities without relying on third-party APIs. We compared the models with human and TF-IDF benchmarks. The Wikipedia Game format enables robust comparisons across techniques, languages, and domains. No model significantly outperformed humans, although the best models (GPT-o3 mini, GPT-4o, QwQ:32B, and DeepSeek-R1:70B) achieved comparable success. Performance and rankings varied across languages and topics, with implications for practitioners utilising these techniques.

3. X-AgentRed: Red-Teaming Personal Agents in Evolving Multi-Service Ecosystems

As the effects of their actions accumulate and interact across services, unintended or harmful outcomes can arise when agents fulfill benign user requests. Proactively identifying these compositional failures requires systematically exploring diverse cross-service paths. Existing red-teaming methods often simply prompt LLMs to identify potentially harmful paths from task and environment descriptions or decompose harmful objectives specified by humans or LLMs into seemingly benign action combinations. 5. MC-EvoBench: Auditing Experience-Driven Self-Improvement in Minecraft Agents

A central challenge in embodied recursive self-improvement is enabling agents to convert interaction history into capabilities that persist beyond one world and transfer to later tasks. 1. Goal-Oriented Bundle Management and Flow Control for Deep-Space Communications via Reinforcement Learning

深空通信处于间歇连接、长传播时延和中继存储受限的环境，Bundle Protocol 的存储-携带-转发要求中继节点在有限存储与稀缺接触机会之间做取舍，而 FIFO 等传统调度器并不显式优化任务效用，对来自不同行星的异构信源还可能造成服务不均。这篇由 4 位作者完成的论文提出一套面向接触驱动深空网络的闭环建模与学习框架：月球与火星的更新经带共享缓冲区的中继转发至地球，用超额年龄 Δ_i^e(t)=[Δ_i(t)-d_i(t)]^+ 刻画目的端信息新鲜度，并据此定义支持信源相关非线性惩罚的目标导向损失，使优先级可按任务相关性排序。方法上，中继控制被形式化为约束半马尔可夫决策过程（CSMDP），用带掩码的拉格朗日 PPO 求解，可行性感知的动作掩码将传输限制在剩余接触窗口内能够完成的束上，仿真基于 DTNSim。这意味着深空骨干网的中继调度逻辑可能从“尽力而为”转向由任务目标驱动的自主决策，若该框架在更复杂拓扑下依然可用，则有限接触与存储资源有望优先服务于高价值科学数据。
